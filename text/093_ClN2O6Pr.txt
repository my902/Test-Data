PrN2O6Cl crystallizes in the monoclinic C2/c space group. Pr(1) is bonded in a 10-coordinate geometry to two equivalent O(1), two equivalent O(2), four equivalent O(3), and two equivalent Cl(1) atoms. Both Pr(1)-O(1) bond lengths are 2.63 Å. Both Pr(1)-O(2) bond lengths are 2.55 Å. There are two shorter (2.67 Å) and two longer (2.83 Å) Pr(1)-O(3) bond lengths. Both Pr(1)-Cl(1) bond lengths are 2.91 Å. N(1) is bonded in a trigonal planar geometry to one O(1), one O(2), and one O(3) atom. The N(1)-O(1) bond length is 1.24 Å. The N(1)-O(2) bond length is 1.27 Å. The N(1)-O(3) bond length is 1.29 Å. There are three inequivalent O sites. In the first O site, O(1) is bonded in a distorted single-bond geometry to one Pr(1) and one N(1) atom. In the second O site, O(2) is bonded in a distorted single-bond geometry to one Pr(1) and one N(1) atom. In the third O site, O(3) is bonded in a distorted single-bond geometry to two equivalent Pr(1) and one N(1) atom. Cl(1) is bonded in a bent 150 degrees geometry to two equivalent Pr(1) atoms.
