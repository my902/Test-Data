NaVTc2Cl crystallizes in the tetragonal P4/mmm space group. Na(1) is bonded to eight equivalent Tc(1) and four equivalent Cl(1) atoms to form distorted NaTc8Cl4 cuboctahedra that share corners with twelve equivalent Na(1)Tc8Cl4 cuboctahedra, faces with six equivalent Na(1)Tc8Cl4 cuboctahedra, and faces with eight equivalent V(1)Tc4Cl2 octahedra. All Na(1)-Tc(1) bond lengths are 3.48 Å. All Na(1)-Cl(1) bond lengths are 3.21 Å. V(1) is bonded to four equivalent Tc(1) and two equivalent Cl(1) atoms to form VTc4Cl2 octahedra that share corners with six equivalent V(1)Tc4Cl2 octahedra and faces with eight equivalent Na(1)Tc8Cl4 cuboctahedra. The corner-sharing octahedra are not tilted. All V(1)-Tc(1) bond lengths are 2.27 Å. Both V(1)-Cl(1) bond lengths are 2.64 Å. Tc(1) is bonded in a distorted linear geometry to four equivalent Na(1) and two equivalent V(1) atoms. Cl(1) is bonded to four equivalent Na(1) and two equivalent V(1) atoms to form a mixture of corner and edge-sharing ClNa4V2 octahedra. The corner-sharing octahedra are not tilted.
