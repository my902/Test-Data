AcRuH4 crystallizes in the monoclinic C2/c space group. Ac(1) is bonded in a 10-coordinate geometry to four equivalent H(1) and six equivalent H(2) atoms. All Ac(1)-H(1) bond lengths are 2.75 Å. There are two shorter (2.42 Å) and four longer (2.56 Å) Ac(1)-H(2) bond lengths. Ru(1) is bonded to two equivalent H(2) and four equivalent H(1) atoms to form corner-sharing RuH6 octahedra. The corner-sharing octahedra are not tilted. Both Ru(1)-H(2) bond lengths are 1.69 Å. All Ru(1)-H(1) bond lengths are 1.93 Å. There are two inequivalent H sites. In the first H site, H(2) is bonded to three equivalent Ac(1) and one Ru(1) atom to form a mixture of corner and edge-sharing HAc3Ru tetrahedra. In the second H site, H(1) is bonded in a square co-planar geometry to two equivalent Ac(1) and two equivalent Ru(1) atoms.
