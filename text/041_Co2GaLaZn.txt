LaCo2GaZn crystallizes in the tetragonal P4/mmm space group. The structure consists of one Zn cluster inside a LaCo2Ga framework. In the Zn cluster, Zn(1) is bonded in a distorted square co-planar geometry to  atoms. In the LaCo2Ga framework, La(1) is bonded to four equivalent Co(1) and two equivalent Ga(1) atoms to form corner-sharing LaGa2Co4 octahedra. The corner-sharing octahedra are not tilted. All La(1)-Co(1) bond lengths are 2.28 Å. Both La(1)-Ga(1) bond lengths are 2.89 Å. Co(1) is bonded in a linear geometry to two equivalent La(1) atoms. Ga(1) is bonded in a linear geometry to two equivalent La(1) atoms.
