MgCo2NiN is (Cubic) Perovskite-derived structured and crystallizes in the tetragonal P4/mmm space group. Mg(1) is bonded to eight equivalent Co(1) and four equivalent Ni(1) atoms to form MgCo8Ni4 cuboctahedra that share corners with twelve equivalent Mg(1)Co8Ni4 cuboctahedra, faces with six equivalent Mg(1)Co8Ni4 cuboctahedra, and faces with eight equivalent N(1)Co4Ni2 octahedra. All Mg(1)-Co(1) bond lengths are 2.69 Å. All Mg(1)-Ni(1) bond lengths are 2.67 Å. Co(1) is bonded in a linear geometry to four equivalent Mg(1) and two equivalent N(1) atoms. Both Co(1)-N(1) bond lengths are 1.89 Å. Ni(1) is bonded in a linear geometry to four equivalent Mg(1) and two equivalent N(1) atoms. Both Ni(1)-N(1) bond lengths are 1.92 Å. N(1) is bonded to four equivalent Co(1) and two equivalent Ni(1) atoms to form NCo4Ni2 octahedra that share corners with six equivalent N(1)Co4Ni2 octahedra and faces with eight equivalent Mg(1)Co8Ni4 cuboctahedra. The corner-sharing octahedra are not tilted.
