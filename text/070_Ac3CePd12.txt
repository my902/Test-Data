Ac3CePd12 is Uranium Silicide-derived structured and crystallizes in the tetragonal P4/mmm space group. There are two inequivalent Ac sites. In the first Ac site, Ac(1) is bonded to four equivalent Pd(2), four equivalent Pd(4), and four equivalent Pd(5) atoms to form AcPd12 cuboctahedra that share corners with four equivalent Ac(1)Pd12 cuboctahedra, corners with four equivalent Ac(2)Pd12 cuboctahedra, corners with four equivalent Ce(1)Pd12 cuboctahedra, edges with four equivalent Pd(1)Ce4Pd8 cuboctahedra,  a faceface with one Ac(2)Pd12 cuboctahedra,  a faceface with one Ce(1)Pd12 cuboctahedra, and faces with four equivalent Ac(1)Pd12 cuboctahedra. All Ac(1)-Pd(2) bond lengths are 3.02 Å. All Ac(1)-Pd(4) bond lengths are 3.10 Å. All Ac(1)-Pd(5) bond lengths are 3.03 Å. In the second Ac site, Ac(2) is bonded to four equivalent Pd(3) and eight equivalent Pd(5) atoms to form a mixture of face and corner-sharing AcPd12 cuboctahedra. All Ac(2)-Pd(3) bond lengths are 3.02 Å. All Ac(2)-Pd(5) bond lengths are 3.06 Å. Ce(1) is bonded to four equivalent Pd(1) and eight equivalent Pd(4) atoms to form CePd12 cuboctahedra that share corners with four equivalent Ce(1)Pd12 cuboctahedra, corners with eight equivalent Ac(1)Pd12 cuboctahedra, faces with two equivalent Ac(1)Pd12 cuboctahedra, faces with four equivalent Ce(1)Pd12 cuboctahedra, and faces with four equivalent Pd(1)Ce4Pd8 cuboctahedra. All Ce(1)-Pd(1) bond lengths are 3.02 Å. All Ce(1)-Pd(4) bond lengths are 2.90 Å. There are five inequivalent Pd sites. In the first Pd site, Pd(1) is bonded to four equivalent Ce(1) and eight equivalent Pd(4) atoms to form distorted PdCe4Pd8 cuboctahedra that share corners with four equivalent Pd(1)Ce4Pd8 cuboctahedra, edges with eight equivalent Ac(1)Pd12 cuboctahedra, faces with four equivalent Ce(1)Pd12 cuboctahedra, and faces with four equivalent Pd(1)Ce4Pd8 cuboctahedra. All Pd(1)-Pd(4) bond lengths are 2.90 Å. In the second Pd site, Pd(2) is bonded in a distorted rectangular see-saw-like geometry to four equivalent Ac(1) atoms. In the third Pd site, Pd(3) is bonded in a distorted square co-planar geometry to four equivalent Ac(2) atoms. In the fourth Pd site, Pd(4) is bonded in a distorted rectangular see-saw-like geometry to two equivalent Ac(1), two equivalent Ce(1), and two equivalent Pd(1) atoms. In the fifth Pd site, Pd(5) is bonded in a distorted rectangular see-saw-like geometry to two equivalent Ac(1) and two equivalent Ac(2) atoms.
