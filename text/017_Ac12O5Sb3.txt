Ac12Sb3O5 crystallizes in the cubic Ia-3d space group. Ac(1) is bonded in a 2-coordinate geometry to two equivalent Sb(1), one O(1), and one O(2) atom. There is one shorter (3.43 Å) and one longer (3.53 Å) Ac(1)-Sb(1) bond length. The Ac(1)-O(1) bond length is 2.45 Å. The Ac(1)-O(2) bond length is 2.80 Å. Sb(1) is bonded in a distorted body-centered cubic geometry to eight equivalent Ac(1) atoms. There are two inequivalent O sites. In the first O site, O(2) is bonded to six equivalent Ac(1) atoms to form corner-sharing OAc6 octahedra. In the second O site, O(1) is bonded to four equivalent Ac(1) atoms to form corner-sharing OAc4 tetrahedra. The corner-sharing octahedral tilt angles are 47°.
