Ac(GaSn)3 crystallizes in the cubic Pm-3n space group. Ac(1) is bonded to twelve equivalent Sn(1) atoms to form a mixture of distorted face and edge-sharing AcSn12 cuboctahedra. All Ac(1)-Sn(1) bond lengths are 4.21 Å. Ga(1) is bonded in a 4-coordinate geometry to four equivalent Sn(1) atoms. All Ga(1)-Sn(1) bond lengths are 2.66 Å. Sn(1) is bonded in a 4-coordinate geometry to four equivalent Ac(1) and four equivalent Ga(1) atoms.
