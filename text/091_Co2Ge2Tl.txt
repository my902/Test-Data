Tl(CoGe)2 crystallizes in the tetragonal P4/mbm space group. Co(1) is bonded in a 6-coordinate geometry to four equivalent Tl(1) and six equivalent Ge(1) atoms. All Co(1)-Tl(1) bond lengths are 3.23 Å. There are four shorter (2.52 Å) and two longer (2.73 Å) Co(1)-Ge(1) bond lengths. Tl(1) is bonded in a 14-coordinate geometry to eight equivalent Co(1), two equivalent Tl(1), and four equivalent Ge(1) atoms. Both Tl(1)-Tl(1) bond lengths are 2.77 Å. All Tl(1)-Ge(1) bond lengths are 2.91 Å. Ge(1) is bonded in a 8-coordinate geometry to six equivalent Co(1) and two equivalent Tl(1) atoms.
