NaLiCo2N crystallizes in the tetragonal P4/mmm space group. Na(1) is bonded to four equivalent Li(1) and eight equivalent Co(1) atoms to form NaLi4Co8 cuboctahedra that share corners with twelve equivalent Na(1)Li4Co8 cuboctahedra, faces with six equivalent Na(1)Li4Co8 cuboctahedra, and faces with eight equivalent N(1)Li2Co4 octahedra. All Na(1)-Li(1) bond lengths are 2.61 Å. All Na(1)-Co(1) bond lengths are 2.85 Å. Li(1) is bonded in a linear geometry to four equivalent Na(1) and two equivalent N(1) atoms. Both Li(1)-N(1) bond lengths are 2.17 Å. Co(1) is bonded in a linear geometry to four equivalent Na(1) and two equivalent N(1) atoms. Both Co(1)-N(1) bond lengths are 1.84 Å. N(1) is bonded to two equivalent Li(1) and four equivalent Co(1) atoms to form NLi2Co4 octahedra that share corners with six equivalent N(1)Li2Co4 octahedra and faces with eight equivalent Na(1)Li4Co8 cuboctahedra. The corner-sharing octahedra are not tilted.
