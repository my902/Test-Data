KAg2B is Heusler structured and crystallizes in the cubic Fm-3m space group. K(1) is bonded in a distorted body-centered cubic geometry to eight equivalent Ag(1) and six equivalent B(1) atoms. All K(1)-Ag(1) bond lengths are 2.96 Å. All K(1)-B(1) bond lengths are 3.42 Å. Ag(1) is bonded to four equivalent K(1) and four equivalent B(1) atoms to form a mixture of distorted edge, face, and corner-sharing AgK4B4 tetrahedra. All Ag(1)-B(1) bond lengths are 2.96 Å. B(1) is bonded in a 14-coordinate geometry to six equivalent K(1) and eight equivalent Ag(1) atoms.
