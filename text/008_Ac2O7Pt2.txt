Ac2Pt2O7 crystallizes in the cubic Fd-3m space group. Ac(1) is bonded to two equivalent O(2) and six equivalent O(1) atoms to form distorted AcO8 hexagonal bipyramids that share edges with six equivalent Ac(1)O8 hexagonal bipyramids and edges with six equivalent Pt(1)O6 octahedra. Both Ac(1)-O(2) bond lengths are 2.35 Å. All Ac(1)-O(1) bond lengths are 2.67 Å. Pt(1) is bonded to six equivalent O(1) atoms to form PtO6 octahedra that share corners with six equivalent Pt(1)O6 octahedra and edges with six equivalent Ac(1)O8 hexagonal bipyramids. The corner-sharing octahedral tilt angles are 48°. All Pt(1)-O(1) bond lengths are 2.10 Å. There are two inequivalent O sites. In the first O site, O(1) is bonded to two equivalent Ac(1) and two equivalent Pt(1) atoms to form distorted OAc2Pt2 tetrahedra that share corners with two equivalent O(2)Ac4 tetrahedra, corners with twelve equivalent O(1)Ac2Pt2 tetrahedra,  an edgeedge with one O(2)Ac4 tetrahedra, and edges with four equivalent O(1)Ac2Pt2 tetrahedra. In the second O site, O(2) is bonded to four equivalent Ac(1) atoms to form OAc4 tetrahedra that share corners with four equivalent O(2)Ac4 tetrahedra, corners with twelve equivalent O(1)Ac2Pt2 tetrahedra, and edges with six equivalent O(1)Ac2Pt2 tetrahedra.
