Ac4LaIn crystallizes in the cubic F-43m space group. Ac(1) is bonded to six equivalent Ac(1), three equivalent La(1), and three equivalent In(1) atoms to form a mixture of distorted face, corner, and edge-sharing AcAc6La3In3 cuboctahedra. There are three shorter (3.57 Å) and three longer (3.59 Å) Ac(1)-Ac(1) bond lengths. All Ac(1)-La(1) bond lengths are 4.19 Å. All Ac(1)-In(1) bond lengths are 4.19 Å. La(1) is bonded in a 12-coordinate geometry to twelve equivalent Ac(1) atoms. In(1) is bonded in a 12-coordinate geometry to twelve equivalent Ac(1) atoms.
