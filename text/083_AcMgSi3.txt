MgAcSi3 crystallizes in the orthorhombic Cmcm space group. Mg(1) is bonded in a 7-coordinate geometry to two equivalent Si(1) and five equivalent Si(3) atoms. Both Mg(1)-Si(1) bond lengths are 2.86 Å. There is one shorter (2.66 Å) and four longer (3.07 Å) Mg(1)-Si(3) bond lengths. Ac(1) is bonded in a 12-coordinate geometry to two equivalent Si(3), four equivalent Si(1), and six equivalent Si(2) atoms. Both Ac(1)-Si(3) bond lengths are 3.41 Å. All Ac(1)-Si(1) bond lengths are 3.28 Å. There are four shorter (3.27 Å) and two longer (3.35 Å) Ac(1)-Si(2) bond lengths. There are three inequivalent Si sites. In the first Si site, Si(1) is bonded in a 9-coordinate geometry to two equivalent Mg(1), four equivalent Ac(1), one Si(2), and two equivalent Si(3) atoms. The Si(1)-Si(2) bond length is 2.50 Å. Both Si(1)-Si(3) bond lengths are 2.43 Å. In the second Si site, Si(2) is bonded in a 9-coordinate geometry to six equivalent Ac(1), one Si(1), and two equivalent Si(2) atoms. Both Si(2)-Si(2) bond lengths are 2.41 Å. In the third Si site, Si(3) is bonded in a 9-coordinate geometry to five equivalent Mg(1), two equivalent Ac(1), and two equivalent Si(1) atoms.
