Lu2TmC crystallizes in the trigonal R-3m space group. Lu(1) is bonded in a 3-coordinate geometry to one Tm(1) and three equivalent C(1) atoms. The Lu(1)-Tm(1) bond length is 3.37 Å. All Lu(1)-C(1) bond lengths are 2.38 Å. Tm(1) is bonded to two equivalent Lu(1) and six equivalent Tm(1) atoms to form TmTm6Lu2 hexagonal bipyramids that share corners with six equivalent Tm(1)Tm6Lu2 hexagonal bipyramids, corners with six equivalent C(1)Lu6 octahedra, and edges with twelve equivalent Tm(1)Tm6Lu2 hexagonal bipyramids. The corner-sharing octahedral tilt angles are 57°. All Tm(1)-Tm(1) bond lengths are 3.46 Å. C(1) is bonded to six equivalent Lu(1) atoms to form CLu6 octahedra that share corners with six equivalent Tm(1)Tm6Lu2 hexagonal bipyramids and edges with six equivalent C(1)Lu6 octahedra.
