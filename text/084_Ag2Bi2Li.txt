Li(AgBi)2 crystallizes in the tetragonal P4/mbm space group. Li(1) is bonded in a square co-planar geometry to four equivalent Ag(1) atoms. All Li(1)-Ag(1) bond lengths are 2.86 Å. Ag(1) is bonded in a 9-coordinate geometry to two equivalent Li(1), one Ag(1), and six equivalent Bi(1) atoms. The Ag(1)-Ag(1) bond length is 2.82 Å. There are two shorter (2.97 Å) and four longer (3.18 Å) Ag(1)-Bi(1) bond lengths. Bi(1) is bonded in a 6-coordinate geometry to six equivalent Ag(1) atoms.
