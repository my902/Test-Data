AcSbO crystallizes in the hexagonal P-6m2 space group. Ac(1) is bonded in a trigonal planar geometry to six equivalent Sb(1) and three equivalent O(1) atoms. All Ac(1)-Sb(1) bond lengths are 3.87 Å. All Ac(1)-O(1) bond lengths are 2.34 Å. Sb(1) is bonded in a 6-coordinate geometry to six equivalent Ac(1) atoms. O(1) is bonded in a trigonal planar geometry to three equivalent Ac(1) atoms.
