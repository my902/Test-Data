MgTbY2C crystallizes in the tetragonal P4/mmm space group. Mg(1) is bonded to four equivalent Tb(1) and eight equivalent Y(1) atoms to form MgTb4Y8 cuboctahedra that share corners with twelve equivalent Mg(1)Tb4Y8 cuboctahedra, faces with six equivalent Mg(1)Tb4Y8 cuboctahedra, and faces with eight equivalent C(1)Tb2Y4 octahedra. All Mg(1)-Tb(1) bond lengths are 3.52 Å. All Mg(1)-Y(1) bond lengths are 3.54 Å. Tb(1) is bonded in a linear geometry to four equivalent Mg(1) and two equivalent C(1) atoms. Both Tb(1)-C(1) bond lengths are 2.51 Å. Y(1) is bonded in a linear geometry to four equivalent Mg(1) and two equivalent C(1) atoms. Both Y(1)-C(1) bond lengths are 2.49 Å. C(1) is bonded to two equivalent Tb(1) and four equivalent Y(1) atoms to form CTb2Y4 octahedra that share corners with six equivalent C(1)Tb2Y4 octahedra and faces with eight equivalent Mg(1)Tb4Y8 cuboctahedra. The corner-sharing octahedra are not tilted.
