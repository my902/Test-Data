Ac3Pm5Se12 crystallizes in the cubic Ia-3d space group. Ac(1) is bonded in a body-centered cubic geometry to eight equivalent Se(1) atoms. There are four shorter (3.27 Å) and four longer (3.33 Å) Ac(1)-Se(1) bond lengths. There are two inequivalent Pm sites. In the first Pm site, Pm(1) is bonded to four equivalent Se(1) atoms to form corner-sharing PmSe4 tetrahedra. The corner-sharing octahedral tilt angles are 61°. All Pm(1)-Se(1) bond lengths are 2.76 Å. In the second Pm site, Pm(2) is bonded to six equivalent Se(1) atoms to form corner-sharing PmSe6 octahedra. All Pm(2)-Se(1) bond lengths are 2.95 Å. Se(1) is bonded to two equivalent Ac(1), one Pm(1), and one Pm(2) atom to form a mixture of distorted corner and edge-sharing SeAc2Pm2 tetrahedra.
