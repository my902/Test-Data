KMg2Cl crystallizes in the tetragonal I-42d space group. K(1) is bonded in a distorted linear geometry to two equivalent Cl(1) atoms. Both K(1)-Cl(1) bond lengths are 3.03 Å. Mg(1) is bonded in a distorted water-like geometry to two equivalent Cl(1) atoms. Both Mg(1)-Cl(1) bond lengths are 3.07 Å. Cl(1) is bonded to two equivalent K(1) and four equivalent Mg(1) atoms to form distorted corner-sharing ClK2Mg4 octahedra. The corner-sharing octahedral tilt angles range from 0-74°.
