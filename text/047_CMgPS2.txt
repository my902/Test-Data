MgCPS2 crystallizes in the tetragonal P4/mmm space group. Mg(1) is bonded in a body-centered cubic geometry to eight equivalent S(1) atoms. All Mg(1)-S(1) bond lengths are 2.93 Å. C(1) is bonded in a distorted linear geometry to two equivalent P(1) and four equivalent S(1) atoms. Both C(1)-P(1) bond lengths are 1.83 Å. All C(1)-S(1) bond lengths are 2.28 Å. P(1) is bonded in a linear geometry to two equivalent C(1) atoms. S(1) is bonded to four equivalent Mg(1) and two equivalent C(1) atoms to form a mixture of face, corner, and edge-sharing SMg4C2 octahedra. The corner-sharing octahedral tilt angles range from 0-67°.
