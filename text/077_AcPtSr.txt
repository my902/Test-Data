SrAcPt is half-Heusler structured and crystallizes in the cubic F-43m space group. Sr(1) is bonded in a distorted q6 geometry to four equivalent Ac(1) and six equivalent Pt(1) atoms. All Sr(1)-Ac(1) bond lengths are 3.16 Å. All Sr(1)-Pt(1) bond lengths are 3.65 Å. Ac(1) is bonded in a body-centered cubic geometry to four equivalent Sr(1) and four equivalent Pt(1) atoms. All Ac(1)-Pt(1) bond lengths are 3.16 Å. Pt(1) is bonded in a 10-coordinate geometry to six equivalent Sr(1) and four equivalent Ac(1) atoms.
