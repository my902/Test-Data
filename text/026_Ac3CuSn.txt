Ac3CuSn is (Cubic) Perovskite structured and crystallizes in the cubic Pm-3m space group. Ac(1) is bonded in a distorted linear geometry to two equivalent Cu(1) and four equivalent Sn(1) atoms. Both Ac(1)-Cu(1) bond lengths are 2.88 Å. All Ac(1)-Sn(1) bond lengths are 4.07 Å. Cu(1) is bonded to six equivalent Ac(1) atoms to form CuAc6 octahedra that share corners with six equivalent Cu(1)Ac6 octahedra and faces with eight equivalent Sn(1)Ac12 cuboctahedra. The corner-sharing octahedra are not tilted. Sn(1) is bonded to twelve equivalent Ac(1) atoms to form SnAc12 cuboctahedra that share corners with twelve equivalent Sn(1)Ac12 cuboctahedra, faces with six equivalent Sn(1)Ac12 cuboctahedra, and faces with eight equivalent Cu(1)Ac6 octahedra.
