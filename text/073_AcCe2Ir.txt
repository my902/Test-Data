AcCe2Ir crystallizes in the tetragonal P4/mmm space group. Ac(1) is bonded to four equivalent Ac(1), eight equivalent Ce(1), and two equivalent Ir(1) atoms to form a mixture of distorted face and corner-sharing AcAc4Ce8Ir2 cuboctahedra. All Ac(1)-Ac(1) bond lengths are 3.69 Å. All Ac(1)-Ce(1) bond lengths are 3.76 Å. Both Ac(1)-Ir(1) bond lengths are 4.19 Å. Ce(1) is bonded in a 4-coordinate geometry to four equivalent Ac(1) and four equivalent Ir(1) atoms. All Ce(1)-Ir(1) bond lengths are 3.00 Å. Ir(1) is bonded in a body-centered cubic geometry to two equivalent Ac(1) and eight equivalent Ce(1) atoms.
